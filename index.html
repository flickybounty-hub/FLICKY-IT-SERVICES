How it works

Three steps, from broken to fixed

STEP 1

Tell us the problem

Message us on WhatsApp with what's going wrong. No forms, no ticket portals — just describe it like you would to a colleague.

STEP 2

We diagnose & fix

Most issues are solved remotely within the hour. If it needs hands on the hardware, we schedule an on-site visit fast.

STEP 3

We prevent the repeat

We note what caused it and, where it makes sense, put a fix in place so the same problem doesn't come back next month.

Questions

Before you message us

How fast do you respond?

Most WhatsApp messages get a reply within 30 minutes during business hours. Urgent, business-stopping issues are prioritized immediately.

Do you work with businesses that don't have an IT setup at all?

Yes — a lot of our clients start with nothing formal in place. We'll assess what you have, tell you honestly what's urgent versus nice-to-have, and build from there.

Can you support us remotely if we're outside Lagos?

Most troubleshooting, security and cloud work can be done remotely anywhere. On-site visits are currently focused on Lagos and environs.

What if the plan doesn't fit our size?

Message us with your device count and setup — we'll quote something that matches, rather than force you into a fixed tier.

Can you build us a website too?

Yes — we design and build business websites alongside your IT support, and can set up your Google Business Profile so customers find you on Maps as well.
